01 Before drinking

Myrkl

Main ingredients

  • Probiotic bacteria (Bacillus strains)
  • L-cysteine
  • Vitamin B12
  • Alcohol breakdown: The probiotic bacteria are designed to metabolize some alcohol in the intestines before it enters the bloodstream.
  • Reduced alcohol exposure: If less alcohol is absorbed, the liver may have less alcohol to process.
  • Antioxidant support: L-cysteine helps the body produce glutathione, an important antioxidant involved in cellular protection.
  • Energy metabolism: Vitamin B12 supports normal nerve function and red blood cell formation.

Small human studies suggest Myrkl’s probiotic formulation can reduce blood alcohol concentrations under controlled conditions.

02 Before drinking

Flyby Recovery

You can also take it after drinking, but I prefer Cheers for after.

Main ingredients

  • DHM
  • Milk thistle
  • Activated charcoal
  • Vitamins B and C
  • Prickly pear
  • Other plant extracts
  • Liver support: DHM (dihydromyricetin) has shown antioxidant and liver-protective effects in animal studies.
  • Reduced oxidative stress: Milk thistle contains silymarin, which has antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties.
  • Cellular protection: Vitamin C helps neutralize free radicals that can damage cells.
  • Inflammation: Prickly pear may reduce some inflammatory responses associated with hangovers.
  • Toxin binding: Activated charcoal binds certain substances in the gut, but it does not meaningfully absorb alcohol or reverse intoxication.

03 After drinking

Cheers Restore

Main ingredients

  • DHM
  • L-cysteine
  • Milk thistle
  • Vitamins C, E and B
  • Ginger
  • Prickly pear
  • Alcohol byproduct support: L-cysteine is a precursor to glutathione, which helps protect cells against oxidative damage.
  • Liver protection: DHM may reduce alcohol-induced inflammation and oxidative stress, based mainly on animal research.
  • Antioxidant defense: Vitamins C and E help protect cell membranes and other structures from oxidative damage.
  • Digestive recovery: Ginger may help alleviate nausea associated with drinking.
  • Inflammation reduction: Prickly pear has limited human evidence suggesting it may reduce certain hangover symptoms.
  • Metabolic recovery: B vitamins help the body maintain normal energy and nervous system function.

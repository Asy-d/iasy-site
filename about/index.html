About

Asy Dhaif is a multidisciplinary designer and digital artist passionate about creating dynamic experiences that combine color, animation, light, and sound. With a focus on projection mapping, she has brought her work to conferences, galleries, and music events, crafting animations that have illuminated sculptures and buildings up to 70 feet long.

Fascinated by experimenting with color since childhood, Asy creates animations that celebrate the beauty of individual colors and the way they blend, move, and dance together in harmony. Gradients have become a signature element in her work, creating depth, movement, and emotional transitions.

Through projection mapping, light, motion, and music, she transforms physical spaces into immersive visual worlds designed to transport audiences beyond their surroundings and draw them emotionally and mentally into the experience.

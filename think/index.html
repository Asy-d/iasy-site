Why a framework helps

Slow down messy thoughts

Getting a worry out of your head and onto the screen already makes it smaller.

Make trade-offs visible

Seeing options, priorities and risks side by side shows what you can’t hold in your head.

Reach your own answer

These tools don’t decide for you. They help you see clearly enough to decide for yourself.

What kind of problem is it?

Pros & Cons Good choice. Pros & Cons can help you see what you gain, what you give up, and which factors matter most.

Eisenhower Matrix The Eisenhower Matrix helps you separate what is important from what only feels urgent, so you can focus your energy where it matters most.

Decision Tree A Decision Tree puts all your options in one place, along with their possible outcomes, so you can make better, faster and more informed decisions.

Pre-Mortem A Pre-Mortem asks you to imagine the plan has already failed. Then you work backward to discover why, and what you can do now to prevent it.
